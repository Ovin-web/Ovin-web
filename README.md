## About Me

I'm **Kelvin Dickson Kimani**, an Artificial Intelligence and Data Science student at **IIT Madras Zanzibar Campus**, an emerging AI researcher, and technology entrepreneur from Tanzania.

I work at the intersection of artificial intelligence, machine learning, data science, and software development. My interests include developing intelligent systems, researching machine learning approaches, building LLM-powered applications, and automating complex workflows.

Beyond technology, I'm passionate about entrepreneurship and building solutions that address practical challenges in our communities.

**What I focus on:**

- **AI Research:** Machine learning, predictive modelling, model compression and evaluation.
- **AI Engineering:** Large Language Models, RAG systems, intelligent agents and automation.
- **Data Science:** Data analysis, feature engineering, statistical learning and predictive analytics.
- **Software Development:** Building applications, APIs and database-driven systems.
- **Entrepreneurship:** Developing technology ventures and solutions with real-world impact.
